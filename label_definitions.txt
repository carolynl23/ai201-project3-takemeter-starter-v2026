analysis: makes an argument about basketball backed by a specific, checkable fact (a stat, a contract or salary figure, a named game or play, a quote, a date) where the fact is the reason for the conclusion
hot_take: a confident general claim about a player, team or the league (rankings, best ever, predictions) with no checkable support offered; strong feeling is not support
reaction: an immediate emotional response to a play, game or piece of news that just happened, about what the writer saw or felt rather than a claim about the world
